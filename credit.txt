========================================================
             THE OFFICIAL MINESCAPE RESOURCE PACK
========================================================

Welcome to the official MineScape Resource Pack!

Documentation updated: 13 November 2026.

This resource pack has been developed for the MineScape
Minecraft server, a project dedicated to recreating the
world and experience of Old School RuneScape in Minecraft.

The resource pack is continuously being developed and
improved alongside the server. Textures, 3D models,
animations, sounds, and other assets may be updated,
replaced, or removed as development progresses.

If you encounter any bugs, missing textures, or visual
issues, please report them to the MineScape staff team.
